What is Hangr?

Hangr is a Virtual Enterprise company offering a clothes hanger with steam and optional scent, for individuals and businesses.

Do I have to use scent?

No. Scent is an optional feature alongside steam.

What am I seeing in the studio?

This is a product illustration. The final shape, materials and performance are not confirmed. Choose a front or profile view, or try the five-second steam animation. Scrolling turns the hanger until you choose a view.

Can I explore without animation?

Yes. Choose Still view or turn on reduced motion in your device settings. The page also shows a still image if 3D cannot load, and you can read all the product information without animation.

Can I order from this website?

No. This is an educational Virtual Enterprise website with no real orders or checkout.
