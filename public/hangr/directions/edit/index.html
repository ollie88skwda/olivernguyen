About the hanger

What is Hangr?

Hangr is a Virtual Enterprise company offering a clothes hanger with steam and optional scent, for individuals and businesses.

Do I have to use scent?

No. You can use steam without adding scent.

What does the illustration show?

It shows how the hanger could look. The shape and steam animation are illustrations, not confirmed product specifications or tested results.

How can I take a closer look?

Choose a front, angled or side view, then use Show steam to try the effect. View illustration switches to a still image. The page also uses a still image if reduced motion is on or 3D is unavailable.

Can I order here?

You cannot order here. This is an educational Virtual Enterprise website with no real sales or checkout.
